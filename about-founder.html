The Founder

Meet the woman behind YEP's mission to invest in her hometown.

Y.E.P. - Youth Enrichment Program was born from a deep love for the community and a lifelong commitment to serving young people.

Founder Rosie Cason grew up in Logansport, Louisiana, before moving away for 38 years. During that time, she built a professional career and devoted significant time to youth services, community service, and outreach efforts in other cities. After returning home as a semi-retired professional, she saw that many children and families in the community had limited access to the kinds of educational, leadership, creative, faith-based, and personal development opportunities that can help young people thrive. Recognizing those gaps, she felt called to use her experience, skills, and heart for service to invest in the next generation of her hometown. Y.E.P. was established from the belief that meaningful change often begins by planting small seeds—encouragement, knowledge, opportunity, faith, confidence, and service—that may continue growing for many years.

Through Y.E.P., Rosie seeks to create opportunities that help children and youth discover their gifts, strengthen their character, develop leadership skills, pursue learning, serve others, and envision a hopeful future. The organization represents both a homecoming and a commitment: a return to the community that helped shape its founder and a promise to help prepare the next generation to grow, lead, serve, and make a lasting difference.

“I have a heart for our youth and a desire to plant seeds that will spring forth in years to come.” — Rosie Cason, Founder
